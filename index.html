My Dearest Julie,

Hi, baby kooo! 💗 If you're reading this, I hope you're smiling right now, because that is exactly what I want this little website to do. I know this is just a webpage, some colors, a few hearts, and a bunch of words, but I made it with you in mind. Every little detail is here because I wanted to give you something that feels like a warm hug from me, even when I'm not beside you.

Baby, sometimes I honestly don't know how to explain everything I feel for you. I can be doing something completely normal, minding my own business, then suddenly I remember your smile, your voice, the way you hold my hand, or something funny you said. And just like that, there I go again, thinking about you. 😭💜 My brain deserves an award for bringing you up at the most random times. Like, excuse me, can we finish one task first? But nooo, my heart keeps saying, "What if we think about Julie again?" HAHAHA.

I miss you, baby. I miss being around you, talking about random things, laughing with you, and simply having you beside me. I don't need every moment with you to be grand or expensive. Even an ordinary day becomes something I want to remember when I get to spend it with you. Sometimes the smallest moments are the ones that stay in my heart the longest.

One thing I really want you to know is that I appreciate you more than I can properly put into words. I appreciate your time, your effort, your kindness, and the little things you do that you might not even notice. I appreciate the way you make me feel happy without even trying. You don't have to do something extraordinary to be special to me. You being yourself is already something I treasure so much.

And can we talk about your smile for a second? Because honestly, baby, that smile is dangerous. 😭💘 One smile from you and suddenly I'm acting like the happiest boy alive. The way you laugh, the little expressions you make, and the way you look at me can turn a regular day into one of my favorite memories. You might think you're just being you, but to me, those little things are beautiful.

I love getting to know you more and more. I want to hear about your good days, your bad days, your random thoughts, your dreams, the things that make you excited, and even the things that worry you. I want to know the little details that make you who you are. I don't want to know only the version of you that is smiling. I want you to feel comfortable being honest with me, even on the days when you're tired, quiet, or not feeling your best.

Baby, you don't always have to be strong around me. You don't need to hide your worries or pretend that everything is okay. If you need someone to listen, I want to listen. If you need encouragement, I want to encourage you. If you need a little laugh after a long day, I will gladly volunteer as your personal comedian, even if my jokes deserve a rating of negative ten. HAHAHAHA. 😭💕

I also know that love isn't just about sweet messages, cute websites, flowers, or saying beautiful things. It's also about patience, respect, honesty, understanding, and showing up consistently. I want my actions to match the words I tell you. I want to keep learning how to understand you better, communicate with you properly, and make you feel appreciated instead of taking you for granted. I know I'm not perfect, baby, but I'm willing to learn and grow.

Sometimes I wonder about the future. I wonder about all the little memories we haven't made yet, the places we might visit, the food we might try, the pictures we'll take, and the random conversations we'll have. I hope we get to experience many more beautiful moments together. And when life gets busy, I hope we still remember to make time for the little things that keep us close.

I never want you to feel that you need to be someone else just to keep my attention. You don't need to be perfect, look perfect, or have everything figured out. I want you to keep being yourself, growing at your own pace, and chasing the things that matter to you. I hope I can be someone who cheers you on and reminds you of your worth when you forget it yourself.

And most importantly, I'm thankful to God that our paths crossed. I pray that He guides us in the way we treat one another, teaches us to be patient, and helps us grow into better people. I pray that our connection brings peace, joy, encouragement, and a deeper appreciation for His love. I want us to remember that the best kind of love isn't just about how we make each other feel, but also about how we help each other grow in kindness, faith, and character.

🕊️

"Love is patient, love is kind."

— 1 Corinthians 13:4 (NIV)

May we always choose patience, kindness, and understanding as we grow together. 🤍

Julie, if there is one thing I hope you remember from this long message, it's that you are genuinely special to me. I'm happy that I get to know you, spend time with you, and make memories with you. I can't promise that every day will be perfect, but I can keep choosing to treat you with care, to listen to you, and to make an honest effort to understand you.

And baby, if you ever have a day when you feel tired or doubtful, come back to this little letter. Imagine me sitting beside you, holding your hand, and telling you that you don't have to figure everything out all at once. Take a breath, give yourself some kindness, and remember that there are people who care about you. I hope I can keep being one of those people for you.

I want to keep making you smile. I want to keep discovering new things about you. I want to keep laughing at our silly moments and appreciating the quiet ones. And yes, I will probably continue to miss you a suspicious amount even if I just saw you. Please don't report me to the Missing Julie Department. I'm already their most loyal customer. HAHAHA. 🥹💗

Thank you for being you, baby. Thank you for the time you share with me, for the moments we have already created, and for the chance to know you a little more every day. Whatever comes next, I hope we approach it with sincere hearts, open communication, and gratitude for the good things we get to share.

If I could end this letter by telling you one thing face-to-face, I would hold your hand, look at you, smile like the happiest idiot alive, and tell you: I'm really, really glad it's you, Julie. You make my world a little brighter, and I'm grateful for every chance I get to make you smile, too.

You are thought of, appreciated, and cared for, more than this little webpage could ever explain. 💜

With all my sweetness and kulit,
Your boy, who misses you a little too much ♡
